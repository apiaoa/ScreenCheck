Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Screen Check is a fully offline screen testing tool for Android. This policy applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name com.screencheck.app.

No data collection

  • The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
  • The app requests no Android permissions — not even internet access.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network connections of any kind.
  • The app stores no files or settings on your device.

Because no data is collected or processed, nothing is shared with third parties and no data retention applies.
